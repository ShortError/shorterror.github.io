Last month, at a small wedding, an old uncle leaned across the table and asked the couple, "Do you love each other?"
Everyone smiled. The couple said yes. Glasses clinked.
Then he turned to me — single, tired, on my third cup of tea — and asked the same question.
"Do you love each other?"
I laughed. "There's only me here."
He nodded slowly, like I had finally said something honest. "Exactly," he said. "So?"
I have been carrying that so around ever since.
